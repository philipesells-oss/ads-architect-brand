```
[The number] — [what it means in plain terms].

[1-2 sentences on the situation before]

[What we did — brief, no jargon]

[The result with specific number]

↓ Free audit if you want to know what your numbers could look like.

```

```

Most [hospitality/wellness] brands [common mistake].

→ [CTA: comment/DM/audit]
```

```
We don't. Here's why:

DM "AUDIT" for a free diagnosis.
```

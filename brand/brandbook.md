# 6. IDEAL CLIENT PROFILE (ICP)

## ICP Primary — Hospitality

```
BRAND PROFILE
· Boutique hotels, resorts, glamping (10-50 rooms)
· Integrated hotel/resort spas
· Premium short-term rental / serviced apartments

DECISION MAKER
· Owner / Founder (not a chain — an owner)
· Age: 35-55
· Behavior: Active on Instagram/LinkedIn,
  referred by other owners, delegates operations

GREEN FLAGS (QUALIFY)
✓ Monthly revenue >$15K/month
✓ Has spent on ads before (even if poorly)
✓ Has dedicated marketing staff or F&B manager
✓ Meta / Google Ads account exists
✓ Uses online booking system

RED FLAGS (DISQUALIFY)
✗ Revenue <$5K/month
✗ No website or booking system
✗ Owner won't delegate / wants to control everything
✗ Corporate chain (procurement blocks decisions)

PRIMARY PAIN
"I'm paying for ads but can't prove they bring guests.
I don't know what's working."

BUY TRIGGER
High season approaching + poor results last season +
recommendation from another owner
```

## ICP Secondary — Wellness Premium

```
BRAND PROFILE
· Day spas, medical spas, premium salons
· Yoga studios / high-ticket wellness retreats
· Premium aesthetic clinics

DECISION MAKER
· Owner / Clinical Director / Spa Manager
· Age: 32-50
· Behavior: Focused on client experience,
  grew through word-of-mouth, wants to scale without
  losing quality

GREEN FLAGS (QUALIFY)
✓ Average ticket >$80/treatment or >$200/session
✓ Has existing client base, wants new clients
✓ Has a team (not a solopreneur)
✓ Has or wants online booking system
✓ Understands marketing value, doesn't bargain on fees

RED FLAGS (DISQUALIFY)
✗ Low-price / constant promotions model
✗ Solopreneur with no scale capacity
✗ No online booking system
✗ Expects results in under 30 days

PRIMARY PAIN
"We depend too much on repeat clients and word-of-mouth.
We don't know how to attract new clients consistently."

BUY TRIGGER
New competitor opening nearby + drop in new clients
over the last 2-3 months
```

# 8. PRICING & PACKAGING

## Pricing Philosophy (Neumeier + Aaker)

**The Premium Trap:** Most agencies price on time or deliverables.
ADS Architect prices on **system value** and **revenue impact**.

The client is not buying "Meta Ads Management."
They are buying **a predictable booking engine**.
Price accordingly.

## Price Architecture

### The Acquisition Sprint™
```
PRICE: $2,500 - $4,000 (one-time)
SCOPE: 14-day build, 1 platform, basic system
AD SPEND REQUIRED: $500-1,500/month (client direct)

POSITIONING:
"Start fast. See results in 30 days.
 Optional upgrade to full retainer."

ANCHORING: Makes Revenue Pipeline look reasonable.
OBJECTION HANDLER: "If we can't show you a clear
ROI path in the first 30 days, we'll refund you."
```

### The Revenue Pipeline™
```
PRICE: $1,500 - $2,500/month (retainer)
SETUP FEE: $1,500 (month 1 only)
AD SPEND REQUIRED: $1,000-3,000/month (client direct)
MINIMUM TERM: 3 months

POSITIONING:
"Your first complete acquisition system. Built
in 30 days. Managed and optimized monthly."

WHAT JUSTIFIES THE PRICE:
· 2 ad platforms managed
· Landing page + CRM + WhatsApp automation
· Monthly Revenue Intelligence Brief™
· Dedicated strategist access (Slack/WhatsApp)
```

### The Acquisition Architecture Program™
```
PRICE: $3,500 - $6,000/month (retainer)
SETUP FEE: $2,500 (month 1 only)
AD SPEND REQUIRED: $2,000-10,000+/month (client direct)
MINIMUM TERM: 6 months

POSITIONING:
"Full acquisition infrastructure. Everything managed.
 We become your acquisition department."

WHAT JUSTIFIES THE PRICE:
· All 3 ad platforms (Meta + Google + TikTok)
· Full funnel: landing pages + CRM + WhatsApp + Email
· Monthly Revenue Intelligence Brief™
· Quarterly Strategy Session (video call)
· Priority support + weekly check-in
· A/B testing program
```

## Price Presentation Rules

```
1. NEVER present price without ROI context first.
   Show case study results → then present investment.

2. ALWAYS present as investment, not cost.
   "Investment: $2,500/month" not "Cost: $2,500/month"

3. ALWAYS show the ad spend separately.
   "Agency fee: $1,500/month + your ad budget ($1,000-3,000)"
   This frames your fee as small vs. the total investment.

4. ANCHOR with the flagship first.
   Present Architecture Program™ → Revenue Pipeline™ → Sprint™
   Top-down anchoring makes mid-tier feel accessible.

5. NEVER discount. Offer scope adjustment instead.
   "We can start with The Revenue Pipeline™ and
    upgrade to the full program in month 3."
```

# 12. BRAND VOICE GUIDE

## Four Voice Attributes

| Attribute | Means | Doesn't Mean |
|---|---|---|
| **Analytical** | Speak with data and specifics | Cold or robotic |
| **Direct** | Short sentences, no jargon | Rude or abrasive |
| **Confident** | We know our craft | Arrogant or dismissive |
| **Executive-Oriented** | Business outcomes, not marketing metrics | Boring or corporate |

## Vocabulary

| USE | AVOID |
|---|---|
| System / Infrastructure | Campaign / Post |
| Revenue / Bookings | Traffic / Clicks |
| Architecture | Setup / Management |
| Predictable | Viral / Organic |
| Acquisition | Awareness / Branding |
| Investment | Cost / Fee |
| Engineer / Architect | Manager / Assistant |
| Optimize | Boost / Improve |
| Cost per booking | Engagement rate |
| Reservation / Appointment | Lead / Follower |

## Writing Examples

### Email Opening
```
✗ WEAK:
"Thank you for considering ADS Architect for your
marketing needs. We are a full-service performance
marketing agency that specializes in..."

three specific gaps in your acquisition system that
are currently costing you an estimated [X] bookings/month.
Here's what we found — and how we'd fix it."
```
